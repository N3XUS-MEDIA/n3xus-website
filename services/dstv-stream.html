Live & On-Demand

One Platform, Two Ways to Reach Your Audience

DStv Stream Live is live linear TV — sport, news and appointment viewing — available across Smart TVs, desktops and smart phones. It carries the same broadcast credibility as scheduled television, with a connected-device audience layered on top.

DStv Stream VOD is catch-up content — local drama, reality and lifestyle titles that South African audiences binge on their own schedule. GE local content is consistently the most-watched genre, giving advertisers a direct line to culturally engaged viewers.

Ad Formats

Built to Match Viewing Intent

Every format is chosen for what the viewer is doing in that moment — watching, pausing, or catching up.

Skippable & Non-Skippable VOD Ads

Plays before, during or after catch-up content. Built for reach and awareness at scale.

  • Max duration 30 seconds
  • Viewers can skip after 5 seconds (skippable format)
  • Clickable on mobile and desktop
  • Clear CTA with UTM-tagged landing URL

Pause Screen

A static, non-intrusive ad appears the moment content is paused — reaching viewers at peak attention.

  • Shoppable, actionable moments
  • Non-intrusive and contextually relevant
  • QR code creative strategy
  • No competing motion or sound

Live Ads

Plays around live linear content — sport, news, appointment viewing — for engagement and brand recall.

  • Max duration 15 seconds
  • Viewers cannot skip the ad
  • Clickable on mobile and desktop
  • Best suited to major sporting moments

Content That Draws Attention

Sport Leads Live. GE Local Leads VOD.

Understanding what audiences are already watching shapes where your ad lands and how it's received.

Sport Dominates Live

Sport content is consistently the most-watched genre on DStv Stream Live, driven by the EPL season, AFCON and local rugby & cricket — with strong interest in local and international general entertainment channels too.

GE Local Leads VOD

General entertainment local drama titles top the VOD charts every quarter — culturally resonant content that keeps audiences coming back episode after episode, ideal for sustained brand presence.

Cross-Device by Default

Whether it's live linear on a Smart TV or catch-up on a phone during a commute, the platform's audience moves fluidly between devices — and your campaign follows them.
